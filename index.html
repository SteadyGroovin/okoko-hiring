The concept

Coconut Fusion. What it really means.

The coconut is the main actor here, as fruit and as tree. We take the kitchens of tropical Asia, where the coconut belongs, and mix them with the rest of the world.

Every dish has coconut in it. Every single one. Sometimes you taste it right away, like a curry that arrives inside a coconut. Sometimes it hides in a dip, a marinade or a dressing. You'll notice.

They call the coconut the tree of life. Every part of it gives something, and nothing is wasted. That's how we cook.

WaterTo drink, and to cook with.
MilkBuilds the flavour.
FleshAdds the bite.
OilFinishes the dish.
ShellBecomes the bowl.
Boracay has plenty of good food. We want to be the dinner people still talk about on the flight home.

Behind O KOKO are two partners with years of experience in Asian and international cuisine and in hospitality. We know what a good kitchen and a good bar need. We also know we can't build this alone.

The place

Two floors. Two moods.

Downstairs, the bar.

A craft bar in the middle of the room. Glowing bottles, coconuts stacked on the shelves, a bar that makes things in front of you. Stools you can spin around on. We're open from lunch into the night, and in the evening the music goes up: funk, electro, a full room.

Upstairs, the dining room.

Quiet, cool and candle-lit, under an A-frame window full of palm trees. We want it to be the most romantic dining room on the island. Dates, birthdays, private nights.

Art on every wall. Coconut and botanical prints, work by local artists, little stories to find. Every time you look up, there's something new.

The part that matters most

We want people to know your name.

O KOKO lives from its story. Why coconut. Where a dish comes from. How a drink gets made. Guests hear it at the table, and they see it online.

Our chef and our bartender tell that story. At the table, behind the bar, on camera and on our socials. We build the brand around you, not just around the logo.

So you love your craft, and you love talking about it. A phone camera doesn't scare you. When a guest asks about a dish, you light up.
